Bold Miso Chicken and Vegetables

(1 serving measurements)

Ingredients to Collect:

  • 1/2 lb of Chicken Breast
  • 4 tablespoons of Miso sauce
  • 1 teaspoon of Cinnamon
  • Mixed Vegetables (Recommended Zuchini and Summer Squash Strips)
  • Dashes of and/or to taste:
    • Garlic Powder
    • Salt
    • Pepper

Prep Work:

  • 1. First thing to do is to grab a chopping board and get your chicken ready. Grab your 1/2 pound of chicken and chop it into the desired amount of strips.
  • 2. Prepare the pan with pan spray or butter (and for rice, put water in a pot to prepare it)
  • 3. *OPTIONAL* Before doing anything with the chicken or vegetables, if using rice, put it in the pot once it is starting to boil.

Time to Cook:

  • Step number one to cook is to throw the chicken in the pan, leaving it on low heat on the stove.
    • As it cooks, season with salt, pepper and garlic.
  • When the chicken is almost done cooking, put about half of the miso sauce measured into the pan, and let the chicken soak up the sauce.
  • After letting it simmer in the sauce, add the measured cinnamon on the chicken, and flip the chicken to let the cinnamon settle in.
  • Now that the chicken is almost done, throw your vegetables in with the chicken, and pour the rest of the miso sauce over them. Add pepper and salt to taste.
  • Let the vegetables soak up the miso sauce and brown. After this, the chicken and vegetables should be done.
  • If you have rice, place the rice down first, and serve over rice. If not, then your meal is ready to serve.
